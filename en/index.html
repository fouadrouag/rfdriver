Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know

Why choose RF Driver in the Oise? +

RF Driver serves individuals and businesses departing from Compiègne, Noyon, Pierrefonds, Ribécourt-Dreslincourt, Chantilly and the entire Oise region. We provide transfers to Paris, CDG, Orly and Beauvais airports, Paris train stations, business travel, private events and long-distance journeys by reservation. Our goal is to offer a reliable, comfortable service tailored to every need.

How does airport pickup work? +

Your chauffeur waits for you after baggage claim, with a name sign on request. Flights are tracked in real time. Waiting time is included free of charge for up to 30 minutes after landing.

Can I book a van? +

Yes. Depending on the number of passengers and luggage, RF Driver can provide a sedan or a V-Class van by reservation. This option suits families, groups, business travel, events in Paris and long-distance journeys.

Do you handle long-distance journeys? +

Yes. RF Driver operates long-distance journeys by reservation to Paris, Lille, Brussels, Brittany, the seaside, the South of France, Nice, Courchevel, ski resorts and other destinations according to your needs.

Which destinations do you serve? +

We serve the Oise, Paris, Île-de-France, CDG, Orly and Beauvais airports, the main Paris train stations, Lille, Brussels, Disneyland Paris, Brittany, the seaside, the South of France, Nice, Courchevel and ski resorts on request.

How many passengers can you carry? +

RF Driver offers solutions for solo travellers, families, groups and business clients. Depending on the number of passengers and luggage, your journey can be arranged in a sedan or a V-Class van, for up to 7 passengers.
