Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

Do you serve Charles-de-Gaulle, Orly and Beauvais airports? +

Yes. RF Driver provides transfers to the main airports from Compiègne, Noyon, Pierrefonds, Paris and the entire region. Journeys are carried out by reservation only.

Is the price known before departure? +

Yes. The fare is communicated before booking so you can travel with complete peace of mind, with no surprises.

Can I book a private chauffeur for a full day? +

Yes. RF Driver also offers hourly or daily chauffeur hire for business meetings, private events or custom journeys.

Do you offer a van for groups? +

Yes. The V-Class van is available by reservation for families, groups and business travel, up to 7 passengers.

Do you work with businesses? +

Yes. RF Driver supports businesses, freelancers and professionals with their travel, client transfers, meetings and events. Electronic invoicing is available for every service, compliant with business requirements.

Do you serve Paris and the rest of France? +

Yes. RF Driver serves the Oise, Paris and Île-de-France, as well as many long-distance destinations across France according to your needs.

Is there a minimum fare? +

Yes, a minimum fare applies depending on the vehicle chosen: €25 for Standard, €30 for Sedan and €45 for V-Class Van (pickup and travel included).
